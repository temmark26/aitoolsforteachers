AI Tools For Teachers

AI Tools For Teachers is an independent educational resource focused on practical AI tools, lesson planning workflows, ChatGPT ideas and teacher-friendly reviews.

Our goal is simple: make emerging technology easier to understand and easier to evaluate in the context of real teaching work.

Our approach

We prioritize usefulness over hype. Articles aim to explain what a tool does, where it may fit into a teacher's workflow, and what should be checked before using generated content with students.

What you'll find here

  • AI tool explainers and practical guides
  • Lesson planning and classroom workflows
  • ChatGPT and generative AI resources
  • Free and paid tool reviews

Editorial note

AI products, pricing, quotas and features can change quickly. When a detail is time-sensitive, readers should verify it against the provider's current documentation before making decisions.
